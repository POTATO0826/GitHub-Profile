<details>
<summary><b>⚔️ ThetaDuel</b> — options trading as a 1v1 duel</summary>
<br />

Options trading is a wall of greeks that scares off everyone who isn't already a quant. ThetaDuel turns it into a 1v1 duel: draw a box on a live price chart and the box *is* the option. Its dimensions are the strikes, and the premium is quoted straight off the Thetanuts book rather than invented. Two players draw blind against the same asset, both boxes reveal on one chart, and whoever's lands at expiry takes the pool. Every match is fully determined by (lobby, seed), so a link replays exactly what its sender saw.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📉 ChadBuddy</b> — catches client relationships before they go cold</summary>
<br />

Advisors don't lose clients to bad advice, they lose them to dropped threads. ChadBuddy reads the Telegram history an advisor already has, finds the relationships quietly going cold, and names the exact message that proves it. Every claim on screen traces back to a real quote or it never gets shown. It lives as a transparent pill floating above whatever you're working in.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🐔 Finchpoint</b> — an AI that watches your chickens so you don't have to</summary>
<br />

Coop cameras count the flock and flag anyone acting off, then every morning Finch drops a coop check-up straight into WhatsApp or Telegram: who to check on first, how warm they are, plus a snapshot as proof. Ask it anything about your flock and it actually knows your farm, not just chicken facts.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🤝 MEETU</b> — an AI-powered CRM for independent financial advisors</summary>
<br />

MEETU ingests WhatsApp conversations and news, then automatically extracts the signals that matter (life events, follow-ups and per-client talking points) and surfaces them in a calm, editorial workspace. The goal: advisors stop losing clients simply because a thread slipped through the cracks.

</details>
